Malignancy Screening & Lab Monitoring

Consolidated so you don't have to search — the pre-start screening and the labs to follow, for the peptides that need them.

Before starting — pro-angiogenic (BPC-157, TB-500) & GH-axis (sermorelin, CJC-1295/ipamorelin, tesamorelin), plus Dihexa & the incretins:

  • No active or recent cancer; take a thorough personal AND family cancer history.
  • Confirm age/sex-appropriate cancer screening is current: colonoscopy, mammography, cervical (Pap/HPV), PSA ± DRE, skin exam, low-dose chest CT for eligible smokers.
  • GH secretagogues: check IGF-1 at baseline and keep it in the upper half of the age range — never above ULN. Supraphysiologic IGF-1 is the modifiable risk; there is no peptide-specific tumor marker.
  • Incretins: personal/family MTC or MEN2 is a contraindication; check calcitonin only if there's a specific thyroid concern (not routine).
  • GHK-Cu: the exception — favorable cancer-gene signature; still coordinate in active cancer.
  • Tumor-marker panels (CEA, CA-125, CA 19-9, routine PSA-as-screen) are NOT recommended for general pre-peptide screening — poor specificity/false positives. Screening = history + exam + guideline-based age-appropriate screening.

Lab monitoring at a glance (each card lists specifics):

  • GH secretagogues → IGF-1 (baseline, ~1 mo, q3–6 mo) + fasting glucose.
  • Incretins → glucose, HbA1c, weight, lipids, GI tolerance; calcitonin only if MTC concern.
  • BPC-157 / TB-500 → VEGF only on prolonged/systemic use (watch angiogenic drive).
  • GHK-Cu → serum copper, ceruloplasmin, zinc:copper ratio; watch copper-overload signs.
  • VIP → lipase (pancreatitis) before/during; blood pressure.
  • Thymics (TA-1/Thymalin/Thymagen) → symptom response ± cytokine / CD4:CD8 in complex cases.
  • PE-22-28 → mood/activation; screen bipolarity.
  • SS-31 / MOTS-c → HOMA-IR/HbA1c if metabolic; function/fatigue.
