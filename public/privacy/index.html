Privacy Policy

Compliance-Ready Peptide Program
Effective date: August 16, 2026 · Last updated: August 16, 2026
This policy explains what information we collect when you purchase and use the Compliance-Ready Peptide Program (the “Program”), how we use it, and your choices. The Program is for licensed providers and is not designed to collect patient information.

1. Who we are

The Program is provided by Practiced.Health, an educational imprint of ATX Infusion & Wellness, PLLC (“we,” “us”). Contact: support@practiced.health, Marble Falls, TX.

2. Information we collect

  • Your email address — to create your account, send your sign-in code, deliver your purchase, and provide support.
  • Business/practice information you enter into the tool — such as your clinic name, provider name, credentials, address, state, and license type. This is professional business information used to generate your documents.
  • A device identifier — a random value stored in your browser to keep you signed in and to enforce the per-account device limit.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not receive or store your full card details.

We do not collect patient or protected health information (PHI). Any documents you generate are used within your own practice and systems; patient data you handle stays in your systems, not ours.

3. How we use it

We use the information above to provide and secure your access, generate your documents, process your purchase, send transactional messages (sign-in codes, receipts, service updates), respond to support requests, and comply with law. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Service providers

We share limited information with vendors that help us operate, under their own privacy terms:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — transactional email delivery.
  • Netlify — website and application hosting.

5. Browser storage

The Program stores a sign-in token and a device identifier in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in on your device. You can clear these at any time through your browser; doing so will sign you out.

6. Data retention

We retain account and purchase information for as long as needed to provide the Program and to meet legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations, then delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing support@practiced.health. You may unsubscribe from non-essential messages at any time; we may still send essential transactional messages related to your account and purchases.

9. Children

The Program is intended for licensed professionals and is not directed to children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@practiced.health.
