Biological age (PhenoAge)
—
Read it honestly. PhenoAge is a population-level estimate of physiological aging derived from mortality data (Levine et al., 2018), not a precise age and not a diagnosis. Its value is as a within-person trend over time against an intervention, not a single headline number. Acute illness or inflammation (a high CRP or WBC on the day) will transiently raise it. Re-check on a stable, non-acute day.
Formula: Levine ME et al. "An epigenetic biomarker of aging for lifespan and healthspan." Aging. 2018;10(4):573–591. Calculated locally in your browser; no data leaves this page.
